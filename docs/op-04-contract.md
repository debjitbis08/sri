# OP-04 — Contract withdrawn pending interaction semantics

**Status:** the signed-threshold v0.1 proposal is superseded and must not be
implemented or evaluated. No OP-04 implementation, search, or result was produced.
Its update equations, coupling grid, evidence families, and scheduling gates are
withdrawn as requirements; they prematurely chose signal-propagation semantics.

The current work is the [interaction-semantics comparison](interaction-semantics.md),
grounded in the [research program](research-program.md) and
[foundations](foundations.md). It separates collective admissibility, perturbation
and response realization, and structural learning. Compatibility relations,
including higher-arity relations, are candidates rather than a selected architecture.

A replacement contract must follow evaluation of these semantic choices. Input
and output modules are perturbation and observation interfaces; layers are
response modes, not processing stages or abstraction levels. Do not introduce a
new algebra, enlarge searches, or implement OP-04 in the meantime.

OP-02 through OP-03B remain valid restricted controls. Preserve their certificates,
the scoped OR `D+R=5` theorem, and the unresolved OP-02B target unchanged.

**Authorized worked example:** [collective role binding](mediation-example.md)
implements an illustrative joint transformation following semantic evaluation.
This separate example does not reinstate the signed-threshold proposal or select
a replacement OP-04 architecture. No broader search is authorized by it.

**Next phase:** the [comparative modeling program](model-comparison.md) evaluates
mode definitions, nesting, interaction, substrates, and their bounds before a
replacement architecture is selected. Candidate cards and common tests precede
bounded comparative prototypes; the withdrawn threshold grid is not revived.
