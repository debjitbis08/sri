# A smaller inner state for a family of outer relations

Status: analytical construction, 2026-10-25. Continues the
[cycle analysis](relational-cycle-analysis.md). Supplied symmetry and relation
classes, not learned abstraction, a graded SRI implementation, or a new witness
on the frozen capacity task. Previous artifacts remain unchanged.

## 1. The proposed inward reduction

Consider four binary outer units on a cycle. Their 16 possible patterns have
8 possible edge-relation patterns h_i=b_i XOR b_(i+1): global complements have
the same edge relations. A further reduction identifies cyclic rotations of
the relation pattern. Equivalently, identify outer patterns under rotation and
global complement. This gives four classes:

| Inner class | Outer patterns | Number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| uniform | 0000,1111 | 2 |
| one different | all weight-one and weight-three patterns | 8 |
| adjacent pairs | 0011,0110,1100,1001 | 4 |
| alternating | 0101,1010 | 2 |

Thus 16 outer possibilities become 4 inner possibilities (two logical bits),
instead of keeping four independent edge-relation bits (16 formal states,
8 cycle-consistent states). No outer pattern is prohibited by this reduction.
The discarded distinctions are absolute polarity and cyclic position, not
erased outer activations. A reference pose is not secretly retained at the core.

One categorical inner variable is not one binary unit: it requires four states
or two binary storage bits. A cost profile over it uses four cost entries. These
are separate state/storage counts. There is still only one actual inner level;
this example does not establish an arbitrarily deep hierarchy.

The inner class specifies a *family* of full-cycle relations. For adjacent pairs,
the possible edge relations are 0101 and 1010. Their two scalar low-mode lines
are respectively generated by (1,1,-1,-1) and (1,-1,-1,1). These lines intersect
only at zero. Consequently, one fixed physical-unit zero-mode direction cannot
represent both choices. Selection of position is a real part of interpretation.
Replacing the union with its linear span would admit additional mixtures.

## 2. Quotient and interpretation

Let G consist of the four rotations, optionally followed by global complement.
There are eight group actions, though individual patterns can have stabilizers.
Define q(b) as the lexicographically smallest transformed bit pattern. Its four
representatives are 0000,0001,0011,0101. A fiber F_c={b:q(b)=c} is exactly one
of the classes above. The canonical representative is a label, not the default
outer reconstruction and not a privileged direction supplied as evidence.

Inward reduction q preserves a descriptor t exactly when t is constant on each
fiber. Here it preserves the four relation shapes, but deliberately loses where
the boundary between pairs occurs and which pair is 1. Tasks needing those
distinctions must use outer evidence/preferences. In particular, this is not
a claim that cyclic position is irrelevant to every task.

Given evidence e and a stored outer preference s, define a conditional profile

```
A_e,s(c) = min_{b in F_c, b consistent with e} Hamming(b,s).
```

An empty fiber under the evidence has cost infinity. Choose c by combining this
profile with its stored inner preference; then choose an attaining b in that
fiber. Decomposition is contextual selection, not a context-free inverse.
Minimizers are sets when tied. The reference implementation chooses the smallest
label and then smallest bit pattern; record ties explicitly. This convention
does not claim rotation-equivariant point selection.

## 3. A frequency interpretation, with a crucial representation distinction

We can define modes over functions f on the 16 possible outer configurations.
Let R rotate the argument and C complement it. On this configuration-function
space define

```
L = 3I - R - R^-1 - C,
P f = (1/8) sum_{g in G} g f.
```

R is orthogonal, C is a symmetric involution, and they commute. L is PSD because
L=(I-R)^T(I-R)+(I-C). Its nullspace consists precisely of functions constant
on each class. P is the orthogonal projector onto that four-dimensional space:
P^2=P, P^T=P, LP=0. Averaging over the group is uniform within each orbit, even
when orbit sizes differ, since stabilizer multiplicities are equal within an
orbit. This is why division by 8 is correct for every row.

The possible eigenvalues are 0,2,4,6: R has fourth roots of unity as eigenvalues
and C has signs +/-1. S=(I+L)^-1 leaves P f unchanged and contracts every
orthogonal component by at most 1/3. Repeated filtering tends to P. The checker
will verify the projection identities, nullity, and annihilating polynomial
L(L-2I)(L-4I)(L-6I)=0 with exact rational arithmetic. No numeric eigenanalysis.

This supplies a precise notion of suppressed modes: variations *within a
relation class*, caused by position/polarity changes. It is not ordinary spatial
frequency across the four physical activation values. It uses a 16-dimensional
space of configuration functions, not a four-dimensional physical-unit field.
No expansion-free neural implementation follows from this construction.

For actual hard-evidence inference, averaging costs is not appropriate: it would
mix incompatible explanations. The interpreter uses the minimum within each
fiber instead. If M f(c)=min_{b in F_c} f(b) and U a(b)=a(q(b)), then

```
M U = identity,
(U M)^2 = U M,
M min(f,g) = min(Mf,Mg),
M(f+constant) = Mf+constant.
```

For finite profiles, M is nonexpansive in the sup norm and U M is a monotone
idempotent lower-envelope operation. It is nonlinear with respect to ordinary
addition. Both P and U M output class-constant functions, but only P is the
orthogonal spectral projector. We do not equate smoothing dynamics with exact
minimization or claim convergence of an alternating hybrid.

**Do not combine independently minimized sources.** In general
M(f+g) != Mf+Mg. On two members of one fiber, f=(0,2), g=(2,0) give minimum
combined cost 2, while separate minima sum to 0. Contributions must be combined
on a shared outer interpretation before reduction, or retain enough conditional
information. Otherwise the core would combine mutually incompatible explanations.

## 4. A fully specified stored-default and observation model

Use the following supplied joint energy with hard outer evidence:

```
E(b,c | s,c0) = Hamming(b,s) + 5*[c != c0] + 6*[c != q(b)].
```

The coefficients are derived from bounds, not tuned. An outer preference costs
at most 4. Choosing the preferred inner class, if evidence permits it, therefore
beats any nonpreferred class (cost at least 5). Compatibility 6 exceeds the
largest change in inner prior, 5, so every global optimum has c=q(b). No finite
penalty overrides actual observations. If evidence rules out c0, choose another
class without declaring infeasible outer evidence.

For each c, combine A_e,s(c) with p(c)=5*[c!=c0], then minimize. The neutral
incoming message is the all-zero profile; C(0,p)=p for profile addition. No
external evidence is different from a zero message from the outer units: they
still contribute their stored preferences. If q(s)=c0, no-evidence inference
uniquely returns (s,c0) at zero energy. If preferences conflict, not all local
defaults can be simultaneously realized; report that conflict honestly.

Hand-checkable cases, fixed before running:

- With s=0011,c0=adjacent pairs and no evidence, return 0011 and adjacent pairs.
- With the same stored preferences and observed first two bits 00, return 0011.
- With observed first two bits 11, return 1100, with the SAME inner class.
- With all four observed bits 0101, preserve them and change the inner class
  to alternating, despite its prior penalty.
- Under complete observations, every one of the 16 outer patterns is retained
  exactly and its class is q(b), regardless of the stored preferences.

The first two partial observations determine a unique member within the preferred
fiber. Other queries can have ties; deterministic decoding does not imply unique
energy minima. The relation family and preference mechanism are supplied, not
learned from outcomes.

## 5. Exact inference and cost

The reference realization is finite enumeration of fiber members, accumulation
of one minimum per class, then class selection and reconstruction. It terminates
and returns a global optimum by the compatibility bound above. This guarantees
neither local scalar descent nor unconditioned recurrent message convergence.

For n binary outer units, cyclic rotation plus complement has group size 2n,
and each orbit has at most 2n elements. The number K of classes is therefore at
least 2^n/(2n). The reduction in a single selected descriptor can save at most
log2(2n) bits in the largest fibers; this is not a general route to a tiny core
for arbitrary n. The unequal orbit sizes also mean different classes discard
different amounts of detail. Do not extrapolate the four-state result as constant
width at larger n.

Naively constructing all canonical labels costs O(n^2*2^n) bit operations:
2n transformations, each copying/comparing n bits, for each pattern. Store all
patterns and memberships in O(n*2^n) bits plus labels/indexing overhead. One query
by enumeration costs O(n*2^n + K); profiles use O(K) cost/argmin entries. A generic
joint reference enumerating (b,c) costs O(n*K*2^n) time. These exact enumeration
costs, not linear tree/cycle costs, apply to this checker. The global class prior
couples all relations and invalidates the previous independent-edge elimination.

Dense configuration-space matrix construction/storage uses O(4^n) entries;
naive matrix multiplication costs O(8^n). Group-action application can instead
avoid dense matrices, but still acts on a 2^n-entry function unless a compact
representation is proved. Here dense algebra is verification at n=4 only, not
a proposed scalable deployment. A learned compact encoding would need its own
costs and preservation proof.

## 6. Prospective check menu

The checker is `experiments/inner_relation_quotient_analysis.py`. Before its first
execution, declare exactly the following finite verification:

- Enumerate all 16 patterns and all 8 actions per pattern. Verify four classes,
  sizes 2,8,4,2, invariance, all eight full edge-relation patterns, and reduction
  of 0011 and 1100 to the same class. Verify the two adjacent-pair mode lines
  differ and each satisfies its signed cycle constraints.
- Construct integer 16x16 L and rational P. Verify symmetry, P^2=P, LP=0,
  rank(L)=12 by exact elimination, and the annihilating polynomial above.
  Verify M U on each of the four class-indicator profiles; min/translation
  identities on declared profiles f(b)=binary_integer(b), g(b)=15-f(b); and
  the two-point incompatible-minima counterexample within the uniform fiber.
  Algebraic proofs above establish generality; these identities are finite checks.
- Enumerate all 16 stored outer preferences s, all 4 inner preferences c0,
  and all 81 outer evidence patterns. This is 5,184 inference cases. Compare
  fiber inference against direct joint enumeration of all 64 (b,c) assignments:
  331,776 reference visits, 65,536 feasible scores. The compressed solver visits
  82,944 outer candidates, 16,384 of them evidence-feasible, and 20,736 inner
  profile candidates. Count rejected alternatives as well as winners.
- Check optimum energy, membership in the full optimum set, observed-value
  preservation, exact class consistency, the preferred-class dominance property,
  coherent-default recovery, and all four hand-worked queries. Record optimum
  multiplicities, class changes, and whether evidence permits the preferred class.

## 7. Decision boundary

Success would establish a genuine reduction in inner distinctions while retaining
all outer patterns, a contextual return operation, and an explicit connection
to modes of configuration functions. It would not establish physical-unit
low-pass dynamics, learned relation classes, scalable graded inference, or an
advantage over conventional structured prediction. The next issue is whether
the quotient can be represented and updated compactly without enumerating all
outer configurations, while retaining the information needed for interpretation.
